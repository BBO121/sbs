Greenpeace Donation FAQs

Q1. When is the first donation amount processed after the donation is completed?

The first donation amount will be charged immediately upon completion of the donation. The recurring payment date is the 10th of every month; if payment is not made, it will be re-charged on the 15th, 20th, or 27th of the same month.

Q2. What happens if the payment due date falls on a public holiday or weekend?
  • If automatic transfers fall on a public holiday or weekend, payment will be processed on the next banking business day. (*If the day before the payment due date is a holiday for the Seoul Office, the payment may be delayed by one day.)
  • Credit card payments are processed on the payment date regardless of holidays, but the system processing may extend the payment to the next business day.
Q3. Can minors make a donation?

Sponsors under the age of 14 may make a donation after obtaining consent from a legal representative. For minors aged 14 to 19, regular donations of 30,000 won or less and one-time donations of 300,000 won or less are permitted.

Q4. Can I receive tax credit benefits for year-end tax settlement if I make a donation?

You can receive tax credit benefits for the year-end tax settlement. Please be sure to enter your personal information for the year-end tax settlement after signing up. To maintain its independence, Greenpeace does not accept sponsorship from the government or corporations.

Q5. How can I inquire about sponsorship?

For other inquiries, please refer to the FAQ. You can also contact the Sponsorship Management Team via email at help.kr@greenpeace.org or by phone at 02-3144-1997 (weekdays 10:00 AM–5:00 PM).
